Official widget, wrapped

This page loads Google's own element.js; only the banner is hidden and the picker moved.

For most of the internet's history, language was a hard border. A reader in Shanghai could not read a German forum thread, and a developer in Berlin could not read a Japanese API reference.

The first generation of browser translators worked sentence by sentence, and they were honest about it: you hovered over a paragraph and the original sentence appeared in a small white bubble.

What this wrapper does

It loads Google's official translator, hides the top banner it injects, and drives it from a small pill in the corner. Everything else — the translation itself, the hover bubble with the original text — is Google's own.

Move the mouse over a translated sentence: the little bubble with the source text is the official one.

Use the pill in the corner to switch language, or pick 原文 to get the page back.
